ABOUT LEO INITIATIVE

Who We Are

Youth in Action for Health and Hope

OUR STORY

Young People Creating Positive Change

Leo Initiative is a youth-led, community-focused initiative based in Gombe State, Nigeria.

Our work focuses on creating awareness, supporting personal development, encouraging education and contributing to healthier and stronger communities.

We believe that young people can play an important role in identifying challenges within their communities and taking meaningful action to address them.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To empower young people and communities through health awareness, education, personal development, mentorship, volunteering and grassroots initiatives.

🌍

Our Vision

To contribute to healthier, more informed, empowered and engaged communities where young people can participate meaningfully in creating positive change.

LEADERSHIP

Abba Muktar Muhammad

Founder / Chairman

Leo Initiative was founded by Abba Muktar Muhammad as a youth-led initiative focused on creating positive change within communities.

The initiative brings together young people around health, education, personal development and community-focused activities.

OUR COMMUNITY

Based in Gombe State

Leo Initiative is locally registered with Akko LGA and operates from Gombe State, Nigeria.
