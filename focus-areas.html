OUR APPROACH

Four Areas of Action

Leo Initiative focuses its activities on four interconnected areas that support health, learning, personal development and stronger communities.

01
❤️

Health & Well-being

We promote health awareness and encourage young people and community members to make informed decisions about their well-being.

What We Focus On

  • Health education
  • Health awareness campaigns
  • Preventive health
  • Personal hygiene
  • Genotype awareness
02
📚

Education & Empowerment

We support learning and encourage young people to develop knowledge and practical skills that can contribute to their personal growth.

What We Focus On

  • Educational programs
  • Student engagement
  • Skills development
  • Learning opportunities
  • Youth empowerment
03
🌱

Personal Development

We encourage young people to develop confidence, leadership abilities, communication skills and a mindset of continuous growth.

What We Focus On

  • Leadership development
  • Confidence building
  • Communication
  • Mentorship
  • Youth development
04
🤝

Community Impact

We encourage young people to participate in activities that respond to community needs and promote positive grassroots engagement.

What We Focus On

  • Community outreach
  • Volunteering
  • Awareness activities
  • Grassroots initiatives
  • Community engagement
