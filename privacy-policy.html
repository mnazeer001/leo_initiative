Privacy Policy

How Leo Initiative intends to handle information submitted through this website.

Last Updated: To be confirmed.

This Privacy Policy explains the general approach Leo Initiative intends to use when handling information submitted through its website.

This page should be reviewed and finalized with the organization's appropriate legal or administrative guidance before the website is launched publicly.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled on the website, Leo Initiative may receive information that visitors voluntarily submit through forms.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Skills and areas of interest
  • Organization and contact information
  • Messages and other information submitted through website forms

2. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Process volunteer expressions of interest.
  • Respond to partnership enquiries.
  • Communicate about relevant activities.
  • Manage website administration and security.

3. Website Forms

The website may provide contact, volunteer, and partnership forms. Information submitted through these forms may eventually be stored in the website's database so authorized administrators can review and respond to submissions.

The final data retention and processing procedures should be confirmed by Leo Initiative before public launch.

4. Data Security

Appropriate technical and administrative measures should be used to protect information stored by the website from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

The website's final security implementation will include measures such as server security, input validation, authentication controls, access restrictions, and secure database handling where applicable.

5. Third-Party Services

Future versions of the website may use third-party services such as maps, analytics, payment providers, social media integrations, or other external services.

Where applicable, the final privacy policy should identify relevant third-party services and explain how they may process information.

6. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if required by certain features or third-party services.

Any required cookie notice or consent mechanism will be added when the relevant website features are implemented.

7. Photo and Media Consent

Leo Initiative may publish photographs, videos, or other media relating to its activities.

Appropriate consent and media-use procedures should be established before publishing identifiable individuals, particularly where children or vulnerable persons may appear.

8. Children's Privacy

Where activities involve children or young people, appropriate safeguards and consent procedures should be followed before collecting or publishing personal information or identifiable media.

9. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, subject to applicable organizational and legal requirements.

Leo Initiative should establish its final retention periods before the production website is launched.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, or data-handling practices change.

The latest version should be published on this page with an appropriate update date.

11. Contact

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy can be directed to Leo Initiative using the official contact details published on the website.
